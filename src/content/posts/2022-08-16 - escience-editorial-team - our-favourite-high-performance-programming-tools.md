[**Why use an FPGA instead of a CPU or GPU?**](/why-use-an-fpga-instead-of-a-cpu-or-gpu-b234cd4f309c), by our former colleague [Atze van der Ploeg](https://medium.com/@atzeus), delves into something some of us software engineers tend to forget: the hardware side of things.
